      the core's control slot at the moment the worker's InitTransactions()
      arrives. The worker is then refused with -2 and returns at once, and
      the loop saw "worker finished" and returned null.
    - Measured with a scratch probe (60 iterations, max.block.ms 1000,
      output kept in cp5/r10/race-probe.txt; the probe's source,
      tests/.../ZzProbeRace.cs, was deleted afterwards): 59 times
      the probe was refused with -2; once the worker returned first with -2
      and the same message.
    - Fix, fixture only: RetryUntilRefused(Action call, Func<Task>
      startWorker) restarts a worker whose own outcome is -2. That worker
      never entered the core, and the rejection leaves the core state
      unchanged. It returns (refused, worker).
    - After the fix: R10 50/50, S12 sync 50/50, and 10 repeats x 2
      runtimes green. No production change.
