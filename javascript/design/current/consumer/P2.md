After P2, an app can build a `KafkaConsumer`, subscribe to topics or a `SubscriptionPattern`, assign partitions,
`poll()` records whose keys and values are Buffers over the fetched bytes, pause and resume, call `wakeup()`, read the
live `metrics()`, and close the consumer. Offsets and commits come in P3, the mock in P4, listeners in P5,
deserializers in P6.

### Native (`native/`)

7. `consumer.c`: the consumer.
   - Its handle embeds the producer's callback queue (`callback_queue.c`). Construction is `ConsumerConfig_new`, then
     `KafkaConsumer_new` with NULL deserializers, then `__as_Consumer_err` and the notify hook.
   - One drain loop per consumer; the finalizer destroys only a closed consumer (lines 155, 184).
   - Every P2 call goes through its `_cb` twin, or through its fallible or `_err` form where it has no twin (the
     three getters, `wakeup()`, `metrics()`). All of them are in the header today (PLAN §3).
   - `poll()`'s records (PLAN §4.2): each key and value is a Buffer over the fetched bytes. The batch, one poll's
     records in one native handle, is destroyed when its last such Buffer and its last uncopied `Headers` are
     collected. Where external buffers are refused (Electron, or the test switch), keys and values are copied
     instead: the copy path.
8. `headers.c`: the copy out of a consumed record's headers, taking `record_spy.c`'s reading code, which then calls
   it.
9. `addon.c`, `util.h` and `binding.gyp`: the registrations, the declarations and the new source.
10. Shared, not copied (DoD #6): the config-map code of `producer.c`'s `new_kafka_producer` becomes one helper both
